# Northwind Goods - Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Refunds are only possible for orders that have been delivered. Orders that are still processing or in transit cannot be refunded; the customer should wait for delivery or contact us to cancel before shipment.

## Refund windows

- Change of mind: refund within 30 days of delivery, items unused and in original packaging.
- Damaged, defective, wrong item or not as described: refund within 60 days of delivery.
- Late delivery: shipping fee refunded within 30 days of delivery if the carrier confirms the delivery was late.
  Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## Final sale items

Items marked as final sale (clearance) cannot be returned for a change of mind. They can still be refunded if they arrive damaged, defective or are the wrong item.

## One refund per order

Each order can be refunded at most once. If an order was already refunded, further requests are reviewed by a human agent only if the customer reports a new problem.

## Approval limits

The support agent may approve refunds up to 150.00 EUR automatically. Any refund above this amount must be approved by a tier-2 human agent.

## Exceptions

Requests that do not fit a standard category (for example goodwill gestures, partial damage disputes or chargebacks) are always reviewed by a human agent.
