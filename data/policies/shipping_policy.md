# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the EU. Customers receive a tracking link by email once the order ships. Orders in status "shipped" are in transit with the carrier.

## Late deliveries

If the carrier confirms an order was delivered later than the promised date, the shipping fee is refunded on request within 30 days of delivery. The item price is not refunded for lateness alone.

## Lost parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, a human agent opens an investigation with the carrier.
