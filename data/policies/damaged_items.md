# Damaged Items Procedure

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives broken, cracked or stops working because of a manufacturing defect, the customer is entitled to a full refund of the item price within 60 days of delivery. The customer does not need to return items that are broken beyond use; photos may be requested by a human agent for high-value claims.

## High-value claims

Claims for damaged items above the automatic approval limit are reviewed by a tier-2 agent, who may request photos or arrange a carrier inspection before approving.
